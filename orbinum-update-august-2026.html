Orbinum Network Airdrop Update August 2026: Daily Quests, Referrals, and Testnet Progress

2026-08-11 · tags: testnet, zk, confirmed, update

What is Orbinum?

Orbinum is a privacy-focused Layer 1 blockchain built on Substrate with EVM compatibility. It uses ZK shielded pools (Groth16 on BN254) to hide sender, recipient and amount by default.

Airdrop details

Orbinum has confirmed an airdrop: Season 1 'Genesis Community' allocates 20,000,000 ORB (2% of total supply). ORB Credits earned during the season convert proportionally at snapshot, taken 14 days before mainnet (Q4 2026). No vesting.

How to participate (updated)

  1. Connect wallet at app.orbinum.network.
  2. Link Discord, Telegram and X for a 20-credit bonus (one-time).
  3. Add testnet: Chain ID 2700, RPC https://rpc-1.testnet.orbinum.io.
  4. Claim testnet ORB from faucet (available every 24 hours).
  5. Complete daily on-chain quests: Shield, Private Transfer, Unshield, Selective Disclosure.
  6. Earn referral credits: 10 ORB Credits per verified referral (requires referral to complete at least one quest).

Key facts (as of August 2026)

  • Testnet live since July 16, 2026 (confirmed via multiple sources).
  • TGE planned Q4 2026 (October–December 2026). Exact date not yet announced.
  • Snapshot is taken 14 days before mainnet launch — activity after that does not count toward the airdrop.
  • Streak multiplier up to 1.5x on testnet quests for consecutive days of completion.
  • Referral program: 10 credits per verified referral, plus the referee receives a 20-credit bonus for linking social accounts.
  • Current faucet allows claiming a small amount of testnet ORB each day to cover gas for quests.

Tips for maximizing ORB Credits

  • Complete all four daily quests every day to earn the base reward plus streak multiplier.
  • Invite friends using your referral link; ensure they complete at least one quest to verify the referral.
  • Keep your wallet connected and check for new quest types that may be added as the testnet evolves.
  • Monitor Orbinum's official Discord and Twitter for announcements about quest updates or changes to the airdrop program.
