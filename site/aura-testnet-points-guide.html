Aura Protocol Testnet: Farm Points by Staking (Confirmed)

2026-08-08 · tags: testnet, confirmed, staking

What is Aura?

Aura is a launchpad with a rewards-based testnet. Points are confirmed as reward type; test tokens can be requested every 6 hours.

How to farm

1) Connect wallet at beta.auralaunch.org/incentives. 2) Link X and Discord. 3) Daily check-in. 4) Request test tokens. 5) Stake at beta.auralaunch.org/staking — stake 1 token at a time, each transaction earns points.
