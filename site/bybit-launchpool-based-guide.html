Bybit Launchpool: Stake MNT or USDT and Earn New Tokens (No P2P)

2026-08-09 · tags: bybit, staking, launchpool, passive

What Bybit Launchpool is

Bybit Launchpool lets you stake supported assets (often USDT, MNT, or the listed project's token) to earn a share of a new token's reward pool. Staked funds can be redeemed anytime — it is a low-risk way to accumulate tokens at TGE without touching P2P.

Recent example

Bybit ran a BASED Launchpool with a 3,000,000 BASED pool: stake BASED or MNT to earn a proportional share over the farming period. Pools like this run on a rolling basis; check the Launchpool page weekly.

How to participate

1) Complete Bybit KYC. 2) Hold USDT/MNT or the pool's required asset in the spot wallet. 3) On the Launchpool page, stake into the pool before it fills. 4) Claim rewards daily or at the end of the farming period, then unstake. Note: Russian users must confirm Bybit availability from their jurisdiction before registering.
